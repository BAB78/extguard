The Problem Nobody Talks About

VS Code extensions run with your full system privileges. They can read your SSH keys, execute shell commands, and phone home over the network. The marketplace has had multiple incidents of malicious extensions stealing credentials and crypto wallets. There's no built-in way to audit what your extensions are actually doing.
